About

Hello,
I'm Jaia.

Private Chef
Postpartum Nourishment Specialist
Doula & Yoga Teacher

I believe food is one of the most profound forms of care we can offer one another. My work lives at the intersection of culinary craft and deep nourishment — especially during the vulnerable, transformative season of new parenthood.

Trained in professional kitchens and drawn equally to the intimacy of home cooking, I bring a chef's precision and a caregiver's warmth to every meal I prepare. My postpartum cooking is rooted in whole, seasonal ingredients that support healing, milk production, and the emotional demands of early motherhood.

What I offer

Services

Rooted in care, crafted with intention. Every offering is designed to nourish body and soul.

01

Postpartum Meal Service

Weekly or bi-weekly in-home cooking tailored specifically for new mothers and their families. Nourishing broths, iron-rich dishes, lactation-supporting meals, and warming sweets — all made from seasonal, whole ingredients. Designed to support recovery, energy, and the profound work of early parenthood.

02

Private Chef Services

Intimate dinner parties, special celebrations, or weekly family meal prep. Whether you're hosting guests or simply want delicious, thoughtful food waiting for you at home, I bring the craft of a professional kitchen directly to your table — with a seasonal menu designed just for you.

03

Postpartum Doula Care

Holistic support for the fourth trimester — emotional, physical, and practical. Combining my culinary background with dedicated postpartum presence, this offering brings nourishment and hands-on care together for the whole family.
